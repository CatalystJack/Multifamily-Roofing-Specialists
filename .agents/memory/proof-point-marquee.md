---
name: Proof-point marquee
description: Why the marketing site uses text proof points instead of manufacturer or supplier logos.
---

The credibility marquee should remain text-based until the project has approved, licensed partner/manufacturer marks and confirmed their usage guidelines.

**Why:** The redesign brief called for real proof points but did not provide licensed logo assets or verified permission to display third-party marks.

**How to apply:** Prefer accurate claims already supported by the site, such as licensed crews, supplier relationships, manufacturer-backed warranties, documented field work, and regional coverage. Do not invent certifications or add third-party logos without approval.
